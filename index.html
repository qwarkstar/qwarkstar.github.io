About

A bit about me.

I'm a second-year Computer Engineering & Computer Science student at Northeastern University, splitting my time between writing iOS apps, designing embedded systems, and shipping little things that make life a bit calmer.

My favorite projects start with a problem I keep running into, like falling asleep on the train and missing my stop, and end with something I actually use every day. I care about polish, restraint, and making technology feel calm instead of noisy.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me working on embedded design for the Northeastern Electric Racing club or tinkering with hardware on my desk.

What I'm building.

Two projects I've poured real time into. Software on the left, hardware on the right.

iOS · React Native · Expo Available on the App Store

R·iving. The travel alarm for commuters.

Drop a pin on the map or search for any real transit stop. Lock your phone, fall asleep, and R·iving watches your GPS to wake you up at exactly the right moment. Built end-to-end from the icon to the ad banner. React Native, TypeScript, background geofencing, Apple Maps integration, OpenStreetMap transit data.

Embedded · Arduino · 2025 First-year project

TrashScan. Smart metal detection for restaurant waste.

Prototype smart trash can that detects, retrieves, and sanitizes metal utensils mistakenly thrown away in restaurants. Built with Arduino firmware, a copper induction loop, a block-and-tackle pulley, and a custom 3D-printed enclosure. Dedicated case study site with photos and the full design report.
