I'm a second-year Computer Engineering & Computer Science student at Northeastern University, splitting my time between writing iOS apps, designing embedded systems, and shipping little things that make life a bit calmer.

My favorite projects start with a problem I keep running into, like falling asleep on the train and missing my stop, and end with something I actually use every day. I care about polish, restraint, and making technology feel calm instead of noisy.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me working on embedded design for the Northeastern Electric Racing club or tinkering with hardware on my desk.

Work 02.1 iOS · React Native · Expo Live on the App Store

R·iving

The travel alarm for commuters.

Drop a pin on the map or search for any real transit stop. Lock your phone, fall asleep, and R·iving watches your GPS to wake you up at exactly the right moment. Built end-to-end from the icon to the ad banner: background geofencing, Apple Maps integration, OpenStreetMap transit data.

Work 02.2 Embedded · Arduino · 2025 First-year project

Trash·Scan

Smart metal detection for restaurant waste.

Prototype smart trash can that detects, retrieves, and sanitizes metal utensils mistakenly thrown away in restaurants. Arduino firmware, a copper induction loop, a block-and-tackle pulley, and a custom 3D-printed enclosure. Full case study with photos and the design report.

  • Arduino
  • C / C++
  • Induction sensing
  • Stepper motor
  • UV sanitization
  • 3D printing
